Text lines

    Boot welcome

    Shown for about 3 seconds each time the watch is switched on, in the 40K and Titanfall 2 styles. Press MODE, SET or ADJ on the watch to skip it (the light button is the flashlight and does not skip). The 24H Standard style goes straight to the time.

    This watch does not have the welcome screen yet. Update its firmware with the new files, then reconnect.

    Colours

    Theme colours are built into the firmware. To change them, edit watch_fw/theme_config.h (each colour is RGB(r, g, b)) and upload the sketch again. The style buttons at the top only change this page.
